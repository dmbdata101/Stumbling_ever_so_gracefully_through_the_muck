# REMINDER — Musician Bookings App: Product Strategy Needed

**Date:** April 20, 2026  
**From:** Robbie (Dispatch)  
**For:** Bobby + Dave to discuss at desktop

---

## The Problem

We can't launch with just one product (the scheduling/booking app) and leave the door open for a competitor to build the complementary tools that naturally pair with it. If someone else builds the thing that integrates with ours, they own the ecosystem — not us.

## What Needs to Happen

Before building the website to sell this, Dave and Bobby need to map out:

- **What else do musicians need alongside scheduling/booking?** (invoicing? contracts? setlist management? payment processing? venue CRM? promo/marketing tools?)
- **Which of those could we bundle or build as add-ons?**
- **What's the full product suite look like** — so we own the ecosystem, not just one tool in it?
- **Pricing model** — single product vs. tiered bundle vs. all-in-one platform?

## Why This Matters

If we go to market with just the booking app, a competitor can come in behind us with a complementary product, integrate it, and suddenly they're the platform and we're just a feature. We need to think about this BEFORE the website goes up.
