## Deliverables to build (in rough priority order)

### 1. The Pitch Deck
**Audience:** Potential investors, co-founders, advisors, potentially big partners (instrument retailers, music schools).
**Length:** 10-15 slides.
**Should cover:** The problem, the solution (Pinch Hitter), the market size, why-now, the platform vision (not just booking — full ecosystem per the strategy reminder), business model (transaction fees + tiered subscriptions), competitive moat, traction (when we have any), team, ask.
**Note:** Don't build this until product strategy is fully locked (see `REMINDER_PRODUCT_STRATEGY.md` in this folder).

### 2. One-pagers (THREE of them)
Different audiences need different stories:

**One-pager A — For Venues**
"Find a sub musician fast when your booked act flakes." Lead with reliability, vetted musicians, last-minute coverage. Visual: emergency-room urgency meets professional polish.

**One-pager B — For Musicians**
"Get more gigs. Get paid faster. Run your music business in one app." Lead with income opportunity, professional tools, peer network. Visual: hopeful, ambitious, "this is your business."

**One-pager C — For Bandleaders**
"Build your bullpen of trusted subs. Never get stuck without a player." Lead with relationship management and reliability of the network. Visual: team/squad energy.

### 3. Marketing Visuals
- Social media graphics (Instagram, TikTok, Facebook) — multiple sizes
- Web banner / hero images for the eventual marketing website
- Email header graphics for newsletter / launch announcement
- App Store and Google Play screenshots (when mobile apps exist)

### 4. Product Mockups (for explainer use)
- "How it works" visual flow — venue posts gig → musicians get pinged → musician accepts → gig happens → payment processed
- Mockup of the Bullpen feature (saved trusted subs)
- Mockup of the Box Score feature (gig history)

### 5. Slide decks for specific moments
- Launch day announcement deck
- "State of Pinch Hitter" updates for early users / supporters
- Conference presentation (if Dave ever speaks at a music industry event)

### 6. Eventually — Practice launch materials
When Dave opens his Short Hills PsyD practice in late 2026, Claude Design can produce:
- Practice one-pager
- Patient intake / welcome materials
- Insurance / billing info graphics
- Logo/branding suggestions (though he may want a real designer for that part)
