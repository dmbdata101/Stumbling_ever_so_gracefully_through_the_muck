Our Story

Built by musicians.
Run by musicians.
For musicians.

Owner Ronnie Pitman opened Groove Addict Music in 2020 because the box-store experience was killing the magic. No commission-chasing sales reps. No pushing the most expensive option. Just real players helping real players find the right gear.

Beginners get the same respect as pros around here. We'll set up your kid's first $99 starter guitar with the same care we put into a vintage Gibson on consignment. That's the deal.
